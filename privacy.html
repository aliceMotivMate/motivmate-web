Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 10, 2026

Welcome to MotivMate. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and related services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: When you register, we collect your name, email address, and profile photo (optional).
  • Usage Data: We collect information about how you use the app, including tasks created, challenge progress, check-in activity, and squad interactions.
  • Device Information: We may collect device identifiers, operating system version, and push notification tokens to provide notifications.
  • User-Generated Content: Messages sent in squad chats, task templates shared publicly, and challenge responses you submit.
  • Media: Photos and videos you choose to upload to squad chats or challenge responses.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the MotivMate app and its features
  • Match you with compatible squad members based on your preferences
  • Send push notifications for daily reminders, squad activity, and challenge updates
  • Display your progress, streaks, badges, and achievements
  • Enable squad communication and community features
  • Analyze usage patterns to improve the app experience

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information in the following circumstances:

  • With Your Squad: Your name, profile photo, daily declarations, check-ins, and chat messages are visible to members of your squad(s).
  • Public Templates and Challenges: If you choose to share a task template or challenge publicly, it will be visible to all MotivMate users.
  • Service Providers: We use Firebase (Google) for authentication, database, storage, and push notifications. These providers process data on our behalf under confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase, which provides industry-standard encryption in transit and at rest. We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us at hello@motivmate.com. Deleted data may be retained in backup systems for up to 30 days before permanent deletion.

6. Push Notifications

We send push notifications to remind you of daily tasks, squad activity, and challenge progress. You can disable notifications at any time through your device settings or within the MotivMate app under Profile → Settings.

7. Children's Privacy

MotivMate is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access and receive a copy of your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent for data processing
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

To exercise these rights, please contact us at hello@motivmate.com.

9. Third-Party Services

MotivMate uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase (Google): Authentication, database, storage, and push notifications — Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Google Sign-In: Optional sign-in method — Google Privacy Policy

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the new policy in the app or sending an email notification. Your continued use of MotivMate after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us:

  • Email: hello@motivmate.com
  • Website: motivmate.com
